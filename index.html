About Yomi

I am an education and family development consultant with over 20 years of experience working with children, teenagers, parents, and educators. With a master's degree in education and a background in instructional design and workforce training, I help organizations, schools, and families create environments where people can learn, grow, and succeed.

My work focuses on three key areas: corporate training and instructional design, education consulting for schools, and parenting support for families raising today's teenagers. I am passionate about bridging the gap between parents, educators, and young people by providing practical strategies that strengthen communication, improve learning experiences, and support healthy development.

Through consulting, coaching, and training programs, my mission is to empower individuals and communities to build confident learners, strong families, and thriving organization

Education is the bridge; I help people build it stronger.
